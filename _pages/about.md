I am a second-year master's student in Robotics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). I currently work with Dr. [Jianbo Shi](https://jianbo-shi.github.io/) and Dr. [Michael Posa](https://dair.engineering.upenn.edu/posa/), and previously worked with Dr. [Lingjie Liu](https://lingjie0206.github.io/) at the [GRASP Lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/). I received my bachelor’s degree in Automation from [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/), advised by Dr. [Zhiyun Lin](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/faculties/zhiyunlin-2.html), Dr. [Max Q.-H. Meng](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/faculties/mengqinghu.html), and Dr. [Chaoyang Song](https://mee.sustech.edu.cn/en/jiaozhiyuangong/2978.html).

I am broadly interested in robot learning. My research develops sample-efficient learning algorithms for robotic systems, with the long-term goal of enabling robots to generalize from limited real-world experience. Throughout my research, I have sought to **make things as simple as possible, but not simpler**. I believe general-purpose robotic systems should be computationally compact and efficient enough to operate in real time and in closed loop, without sacrificing their capability to handle complex, long-horizon tasks under real-world uncertainty.
